## Transport Instructions

The machine is supplied in a sturdy cardboard box with polystyrene foam for impact protection.

### Before Transport

1. Disconnect all accessories and connected cables
2. Secure all moving parts with packing tape
3. Protect painted surfaces with bubble wrap

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**Crush hazard.** Always use adequate personal protective equipment. The machine weights (see technical data) require the use of appropriate lifting equipment.
{{< /warning >}}

## During Transport

- Keep the box in vertical position (▭)
- Protect against moisture and shocks
- Transport temperature: -25°C to +60°C
- Maximum relative humidity: 95% (non-condensing)

{{< note >}}
Always keep the original packaging for future transports. Inadequate packaging may cause damage not covered by the warranty.
{{< /note >}}

## After Transport

1. Inspect the outer box before opening
2. If visible damage is present, document it with photographs before signing the delivery note
3. Verify that all components listed in the delivery list are present
