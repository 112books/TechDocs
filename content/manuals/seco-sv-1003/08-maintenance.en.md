{{< warning type="electric" >}}
**DANGER** — Live cables. Risk of electric shock. Electrical installation work must only be carried out by qualified personnel.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**WARNING** — Machines contaminated with hazardous substances. Risk of poisoning and infection. If the machine is contaminated with hazardous substances, appropriate personal protective equipment must be worn.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="thermal" >}}
**CAUTION** — Very hot surfaces. Risk of burns. Before carrying out any action that requires touching the machine, allow it to cool down.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Use of inappropriate cleaning products. Risk of removing safety stickers and protective paint. Do not use incompatible solvents to clean the machine.
{{< /warning >}}

### Pre-Maintenance Procedure

{{< step number="1" >}}
Switch off the machine and **lock it out** to prevent accidental start-up.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="2" >}}
Depressurize connected pipes to return to atmospheric pressure.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="3" >}}
Disconnect all connections.
{{< /step >}}

## Maintenance Schedule

Maintenance intervals depend largely on specific operating conditions. The intervals indicated are **starting values**, which should be shortened or lengthened as appropriate.

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| **Every 3000 hours** | Check vanes (VA) and replace if necessary. Replace felt washers (FW). |
| **Every 6 months** | Clean dust and dirt from the machine. |
| **Every 6 months** (with filter) | Check suction filter cartridge and replace if necessary. |

{{< note >}}
With harsh applications or especially demanding conditions (high dust concentrations, contamination, process material penetration), it may be necessary to **significantly shorten** maintenance intervals.
{{< /note >}}

### Required Tools

| Tool | Size |
|---|---|
| Hex key | 4 mm |
| Washer pliers | 3 mm |

### Vane Replacement Criteria

| Model | Minimum Height (H) |
|---|---|
| **SV 1003 D** | H ≥ 14 mm |
| **SV 1005 D** | H ≥ 17 mm |

If H is below the minimum value, **replace immediately**.
