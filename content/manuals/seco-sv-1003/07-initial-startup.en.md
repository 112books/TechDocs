{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Do not lubricate the compression chamber of the machine with oil or grease.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="thermal" >}}
**CAUTION** — During operation, surfaces may reach temperatures above **70 °C**. Risk of burns. Avoid touching the machine during and immediately after operation.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**CAUTION** — Machine noise during operation. Risk of hearing damage. If certain people must be near a machine without acoustic insulation for extended periods, make sure to use **hearing protection**.
{{< /warning >}}

## Startup Procedure

{{< step number="1" >}}
Make sure the [installation conditions](../05-installation/) are met.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="2" >}}
Start the machine.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="3" >}}
Ensure that the maximum permitted number of starts **does not exceed 12 starts per hour**. These starts should be distributed over one hour.
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="4" >}}
Ensure that operating conditions correspond to the [Technical Data](../13-technical-data/).
{{< /step >}}

{{< step number="5" >}}
Once the machine is running under normal conditions: **measure the motor current** and record it as a reference for future maintenance tasks and troubleshooting.
{{< /step >}}
