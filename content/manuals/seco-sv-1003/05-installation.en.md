## Installation Conditions

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Use of the machine outside permitted installation conditions. Risk of premature failures and loss of efficiency. Make sure that the installation conditions are fully met.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< figure src="/images/seco-sv-1003/05-espacio-instalacion.svg" alt="Minimum installation space" caption="Minimum space around the machine" numbered="5.1" >}}

### Environmental Requirements

- Ensure that the environment is **not potentially explosive**
- Ambient conditions must correspond with the [Technical Data](#technical-data)
- The installation location must be protected from weather conditions and lightning
- The space must be sufficiently ventilated to allow adequate cooling
- Cooling air inlets and outlets **must not be covered or obstructed**
- Leave sufficient space for maintenance tasks

### Mounting Position

The machine must be placed or mounted in a **horizontal position**. A maximum deviation of **1° in any direction** is acceptable.

Minimum recommended space around the machine:

| Direction | Minimum Space |
|---|---|
| Side | ~2 cm |
| Top | ~2 cm |
| Front (maintenance) | Sufficient for access |

{{< note >}}
If the machine is installed at an altitude above **1000 meters above sea level**, contact your Busch representative, as it will be necessary to reduce the maximum motor power or limit the ambient temperature.
{{< /note >}}

## Connection Pipes

Remove all protective caps before installation.

- Connection pipes **must not cause stress** on the machine connection. If necessary, use flexible joints.
- The width of the pipes must be at least equal to that of the machine connections along the entire pipe.
- In case of long pipe lengths, use larger widths to avoid loss of efficiency.

### Suction Connection

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Penetration of foreign bodies or liquids. Risk of damage to the machine. If the inlet gas contains dust or other foreign solid particles, install a suitable filter (5 microns or less) upstream of the machine.
{{< /warning >}}

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Connection size | G1/4 |

### Exhaust Connection

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Discharge gas flow obstructed. Risk of damage to the machine. Make sure that the expelled gas flows without obstructions. **Do not close or restrict the flow** of the exhaust pipe or use it as a compressed air source.
{{< /warning >}}

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Connection size | G3/8 |
