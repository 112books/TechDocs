## General Review

The general review must be performed every 500 operating hours or every 6 months (whichever occurs first).

### Procedure

1. **Stop and lockout** — Stop the machine and lock the main switch with a padlock
2. **Visual inspection** — Look for oil leaks, visible corrosion, disconnected cables
3. **Oil level check** — Oil must be between MIN and MAX marks
4. **Vibration check** — Measure vibration at motor housing (maximum 2.8 mm/s)
5. **Operation test** — Start up and observe abnormal noises for 10 minutes

{{< warning type="electric" >}}
**Electrical hazard.** Before opening the electrical panel, ensure the machine is disconnected from the mains and that capacitor charge has dissipated (minimum 5 minutes).
{{< /warning >}}
