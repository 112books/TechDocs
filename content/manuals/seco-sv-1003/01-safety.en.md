Before handling the machine, you must have read and understood this instruction manual. If you need any clarification, please contact your Busch representative.

This instruction manual will remain valid as long as the customer does not make any alterations to the product.

The machine is designed for industrial use. It should only be operated by personnel with adequate technical training.

Always wear appropriate personal protective equipment according to local regulations.

The machine has been designed and manufactured using state-of-the-art methods. However, residual risks may remain. Safety notes and warning messages are indicated with the following key words:

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**DANGER** — Indicates an imminent risk situation that will result in death or serious injury if not avoided.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**WARNING** — Indicates a potentially dangerous situation that may result in death or serious injury.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< warning type="thermal" >}}
**CAUTION** — Indicates a potentially dangerous situation that may result in minor injury.
{{< /warning >}}

{{< note >}}
**NOTICE** — Indicates a potentially dangerous situation that may result in material damage.

**NOTE** — Indicates useful tips and recommendations, as well as information necessary to use the appliance efficiently and without complications.
{{< /note >}}
