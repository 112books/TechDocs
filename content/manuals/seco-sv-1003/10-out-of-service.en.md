## Decommissioning Procedure

If the machine is permanently taken out of service, follow this procedure.

### Before Disconnection

1. Stop the machine following the normal shutdown procedure
2. Disconnect the power supply and lock the electrical panel
3. Allow the machine to cool completely (minimum 30 minutes)
4. Release internal pressure following the maintenance chapter procedure

{{< warning type="electric" >}}
**Electrical hazard.** Only authorized personnel may work on the electrical panel. Verify with a multimeter that no voltage is present before touching any terminal.
{{< /warning >}}

### Dismantling

1. Disconnect all pipes and conduits (seal ends to prevent leaks)
2. Drain working fluid following local environmental regulations
3. Disassemble components in reverse order of assembly
4. Separate materials for recycling according to chapter 11 table

{{< warning type="chemical" >}}
**Chemical hazard.** Working fluid may be harmful to the environment. Do not pour fluid directly into wastewater.
{{< /warning >}}
