## Operating Principle

The machine operates with **rotary vanes**. Compression is carried out **without any type of lubrication**.

{{< warning type="general" >}}
**NOTICE** — Lubricating a dry-running machine (compression chamber). Risk of damage to the machine. **Do not lubricate the compression chamber of the machine with oil or grease.**
{{< /warning >}}

## Intended Use

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**WARNING** — In case of foreseeable misuse outside the intended use of the machine. Risk of injury, damage to the machine and environmental damage. Make sure to follow all instructions described in this manual.
{{< /warning >}}

- The displacement of other media generates an increase in thermal or mechanical loads and is only allowed after consulting Busch
- The machine is designed for placement in **environments that are not potentially explosive**
- The machine is capable of maintaining the limit vacuum (see [Technical Data](#technical-data))
- The machine can operate **without interruption**

### Motor Thermal Protection

- **Single-phase motors**: include a thermal protection switch to protect against overloads
- **Three-phase motors**: do **not** include a thermal protection switch

## Optional Accessories

### Suction Filter

The inlet filter protects the machine from dust and other solids present in the process gas. Available with paper or polyester cartridge.

### Hose Nozzle

Depending on the version, a hose nozzle can be fitted to the suction or exhaust port. Allows easy connection via a flexible hose.
