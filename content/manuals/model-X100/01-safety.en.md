{{< warning type="electric" >}}
**Risk of electric shock.** Before any intervention, ensure that the machine is disconnected from the power supply and that residual voltages have dissipated. Wait a minimum of 5 minutes after disconnection.
{{< /warning >}}

## General Safety Regulations

This equipment has been designed and manufactured in accordance with the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC. Improper use may result in serious health risks.

### Authorized Personnel

Only qualified and specifically trained personnel may:

1. Perform installation tasks
2. Operate the machine in production mode
3. Execute preventive or corrective maintenance

{{< note >}}
Refer to the training section in the operation chapter for specific personnel qualification requirements.
{{< /note >}}

### Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

During installation and maintenance, the use of the following is mandatory:

- Safety helmet (EN 397)
- Safety glasses (EN 166)
- Mechanical protection gloves (EN 388)
- Safety footwear with reinforced toe cap (EN ISO 20345)

{{< warning type="crush" >}}
**Crush risk.** Never insert hands into machine movement areas while it is running. Safety devices may only be removed when the machine is stopped and locked.
{{< /warning >}}

## Safety Signage

The machine incorporates the following safety signs:

| Sign | Meaning | Location |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ | Electrical hazard | Electrical panel |
| ⚠ | Hot surface | Process area |
| 🚫 | No access | Inside enclosure |
