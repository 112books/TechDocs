## Storage Conditions

If the machine is not used immediately after receipt, it must be stored correctly.

### Location

- Covered, dry, and well-ventilated place
- Temperature: +5°C to +40°C
- Relative humidity: maximum 80% (non-condensing)
- Away from heat sources and corrosive products

{{< warning type="chemical" >}}
**Chemical hazard.** Do not store the machine in environments with corrosive vapors or aggressive gases. This may damage internal components and void the warranty.
{{< /warning >}}

### Preparation for long-term storage

1. Clean the machine with a soft, dry cloth
2. Protect electrical connections with protective tape
3. Cover the machine with a breathable tarp (not airtight plastic)
4. Place a label with the storage date and planned review date

{{< note >}}
The machine can be stored for up to 12 months without requiring additional preventive maintenance, provided the indicated environmental conditions are respected.
{{< /note >}}

## Inspection before startup

If the machine has been stored for more than 3 months:

1. Inspect the condition of seals and gaskets
2. Check for moisture or corrosion at terminals
3. Verify the integrity of electrical wiring
4. Perform a no-load operation test for 15 minutes
